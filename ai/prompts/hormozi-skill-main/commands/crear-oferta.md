The user wants to create a Grand Slam Offer for: $ARGUMENTS

Follow this exact process:

1. Ask clarifying questions about their product/service, target audience, and current pricing (if any)
2. Identify the Dream Outcome their customer wants
3. List all obstacles between the customer and that outcome
4. Turn each obstacle into a solution/deliverable
5. Design the Delivery Vehicle (DIY/DWY/DFY)
6. Create the Bonus Stack (each bonus eliminates a specific objection)
7. Design the Guarantee (choose the right type)
8. Add real Scarcity/Urgency
9. Name the offer using M-A-G-I-C formula
10. Present the complete Value Stack with pricing

Present the final Grand Slam Offer in a clean, professional format ready to use.
