# Offer Architecture Reference Guide
**Based on Alex Hormozi's $100M Offers & Gym Launch Secrets**

---

## Overview

Una oferta "irresistible" no es sobre el precio más bajo. Es sobre crear un paquete tan valioso y tan bien estructurado que a tu cliente le parezca imposible decir "no".

Hormozi's core principle: **"La gente no compra basado en lo que cuesta, sino basado en lo que creen que vale."**

---

## 1. Grand Slam Offer System

### The Framework

El **Grand Slam Offer** es un sistema para crear ofertas que resuelven TODOS los obstáculos entre tu cliente y su resultado deseado. No es solo un producto; es un paquete completo de valor.

### Step-by-Step Process

#### Step 1: Identify the Dream Outcome (El Resultado Deseado)

**La pregunta definitoria:** ¿Cuál es el resultado final que tu cliente realmente quiere? (No lo que crees que quiere; lo que REALMENTE quiere)

**Ejemplos de Dream Outcomes:**
- Para un entrenador de fitness: "Tener un cuerpo de revista en 90 días"
- Para una agencia: "Generar $100k en nuevos ingresos recurrentes en 6 meses"
- Para un coach de negocios: "Vender mi negocio por $5M+"
- Para un SaaS: "Automatizar mi operación completamente y reducir costos en 40%"

**Template:**
```
Dream Outcome: [Resultado específico, medible, deseable]
Timeframe: [Período realista]
Measurable: [Cómo sabremos que se logró]
```

#### Step 2: List ALL the Problems/Obstacles

Este es el paso donde la mayoría de oferentes falla. No haces un producto; haces una lista de TODOS los obstáculos.

**Preguntas para identificar obstáculos:**
- ¿Qué no saben sobre cómo lograr esto?
- ¿Qué hábitos les impiden?
- ¿Cuál es la emoción que los detiene? (miedo, vergüenza, confusión)
- ¿Qué herramientas les faltan?
- ¿Quién podrían necesitar que les ayude?
- ¿Qué cambios debe hacer en su vida?

**Ejemplo: Para alguien que quiere perder 20kg en 6 meses**

Problems/Obstacles:
1. No saben qué comer exactamente
2. No tienen un plan de entrenamiento comprobado
3. Se rinden cuando no ven resultados en 2 semanas
4. Tienen hambre todo el tiempo
5. No saben cómo manejar restaurantes/salidas sociales
6. No tienen motivación/accountability
7. Tienen lesiones previas
8. Trabajan 60+ horas por semana (sin tiempo)
9. Creen que necesitan pasar 2 horas en el gym
10. No saben cómo mantener resultados después

#### Step 3: Turn Each Problem into a Solution

Cada problema = Un componente de tu oferta.

| Obstacle | Solution | Delivery Vehicle |
|----------|----------|------------------|
| No saben qué comer | Meal plan personalizado | PDF + App |
| No tienen plan de entrenamiento | 90-day workout program | Videos + trackers |
| Se rinden en 2 semanas | Daily accountability | Group chat + check-ins |
| Tienen hambre | Hunger-management guide | E-book + recipes |
| Restaurantes/sociales | Navigation playbook | Quick-reference guides |
| Sin motivación | Coach + community | 1-on-1 calls + group |
| Lesiones previas | Modification guide | Video library |
| Sin tiempo | 20-min workouts | Pre-recorded sessions |
| Creen que necesitan 2h | Proof that 20min works | Case studies + science |
| Mantener resultados | Maintenance program | Continuity offer |

#### Step 4: Turn Each Solution into a Delivery Vehicle

No es lo mismo dar un "plan de nutrición" que dar:
- Un PDF de 50 páginas
- Una aplicación donde seleccionan sus comidas
- Llamadas semanales con nutricionista
- Entrega diaria de comidas preparadas

La delivery vehicle determina el precio que puedes cobrar.

**Common Delivery Vehicles:**
- Digital: PDF, videos, templates, software, apps
- Live: Group coaching, webinars, workshops, events
- 1-on-1: Personal coaching, consulting, done-for-you service
- Physical: Equipment, books, supplements, products
- Community: Group access, forum, mastermind, Slack group
- Hybrid: Combination of above

#### Step 5: Trim and Stack — Keep the Highest-Value, Lowest-Cost

**Esta es la parte mágica.** No incluyas TODO. Incluye solo:
- Lo que realmente resuelve el problema
- Lo que tiene costo bajo para ti pero valor alto para el cliente
- Lo que diferencia tu oferta de competidores

**Pregunta filtro para cada componente:**
"¿Es esto esencial para que el cliente logre el resultado? Si saco esto, ¿se reduce su probabilidad de éxito?"

**Si la respuesta es NO → sácalo (o ponlo como bonus)**

**La meta:** Máximo 3-5 componentes principales.

#### Step 6: Add Enhancers

Enhancers no son parte del core offer; multiplican su valor percibido:

- **Bonuses:** Valor adicional (hablaremos en profundidad abajo)
- **Guarantee:** Eliminan el riesgo (hablaremos abajo)
- **Scarcity:** Limitar cantidad/tiempo (hablaremos abajo)
- **Urgency:** Crear razón para actuar YA
- **Status/Identity:** "Unirte al club de los ganadores"

### The Delivery Cube

El **Delivery Cube** es la herramienta para decidir CÓMO entregar tu oferta y cuánto cobrar.

```
                    PRICE MULTIPLIER
                    ↑
                    |
        [Done-For-You]
           /     |      \
          /      |       \
[1-to-1]--------[Small Gr]--------[1-to-Many]
     |           |           |
     |      [Done-With-You] |
     |           |           |
    [DIY]--------+-----------+
```

**Axis 1: Level of Effort**
- DIY (Do It Yourself): Clientes hacen todo → Precio más bajo
- Done-With-You (DWY): Mezcla de tu trabajo + trabajo del cliente → Precio medio
- Done-For-You (DFY): Haces todo tú → Precio más alto

**Axis 2: Audience Size**
- 1-to-1: Personal coaching/consulting
- Small Group: 5-50 personas, atención personalizada
- 1-to-Many: 100+ personas, contenido pre-grabado

**Price Multipliers** (aproximados):

| Delivery | 1-to-1 | Small Group | 1-to-Many |
|----------|--------|-------------|-----------|
| DIY | $500-$5k | $2k-$10k | $100-$1k |
| DWY | $5k-$25k | $10k-$50k | $1k-$10k |
| DFY | $25k-$100k+ | $50k-$250k+ | $10k-$100k+ |

**La regla de oro:** Cuanto más "arriba y a la derecha" en el cubo, más puedes cobrar y menos clientes necesitas.

### Value Equation Applied to Each Component

Cada componente debe responder:

**Value Equation = (Dream Outcome + Likelihood of Achieving It) / (Time to Result + Effort & Sacrifice Required)**

**Ejemplo de componente:**

*Componente: "90-Day Meal Plan (Personalizado)"*

- Dream Outcome: Comer exactamente lo que acelera pérdida de grasa
- Likelihood: 95% (porque es específico para ellos)
- Time to Result: Día 1 (lo tienen inmediatamente)
- Effort: 5 minutos por día (mínimo)

**Value Perception:** ALTA ✓

## 2. Pricing Framework

### Core Principle: Price to Value, Not to Cost

**El error más común:** Calcular tu costo + margen = precio.

**La verdad:** Tu precio debe basarse en el VALOR que creas, no en lo que te cuesta hacerlo.

**Ejemplo:**
- Un PDF que te toma 20 horas crear = costo bajo para ti
- Pero si el PDF vale $50,000 en resultados = puedes cobrar $5,000

**The 10X Rule**

Tu oferta debe ser worth at least 10X lo que cobras.

Si cobras $1,000 → el valor debe ser $10,000+
Si cobras $10,000 → el valor debe ser $100,000+

**¿Cómo se calcula el "valor"?**
1. Dinero que hace/ahorra el cliente
2. Tiempo que ahorra
3. Riesgo que elimina
4. Oportunidad que crea

**Fórmula:**
```
Valor = (Dinero generado por cliente) +
        (Tiempo ahorrado × hourly rate del cliente) +
        (Riesgo eliminado × probabilidad × costo del fracaso)
```

---

### The Convergence Pricing Strategy

No empieces bajo. Empieza ALTO y baja si es necesario.

**El proceso:**

1. **Calculate the Maximum Value:** ¿Cuál es el máximo que un cliente podría rationally pagar?
   - Ejemplo: Si generas $100k para ellos, podrían pagar hasta $20-30k

2. **Price at 70-90% of Maximum:** No toda la value, porque quieres que sientan que es un deal.
   - Si max es $30k → precio $21k-$27k

3. **Offer Payment Plans to Make it Accessible:** Haz que sea "afffordable"
   - $21k full price O 3 payments de $8k

4. **Only Lower if You Can't Fill Your Cohort:** Si después de 2-3 semanas no tienes suficientes, baja.
   - Nunca bajes como estrategia inicial

**Why this works:**
- Empieza alto = la gente percibe más valor
- Si la gente dice "muy caro" → ahora tienes razón para bajar (y parecerá un deal)
- Si la gente dice "es caro pero vale" → felicidades, encontraste tu precio

## 3. Bonus Stack Design

### The Purpose of Bonuses

Los bonuses NO son "extras cute". Son herramientas estratégicas para:
1. Eliminar objecciones específicas
2. Incrementar el valor percibido sin incrementar costos
3. Diferenciarte de competidores
4. Crear anchors de precio más altos

### Core Rules for Bonus Stacking

**Rule 1: One Bonus = One Objection**

Cada bonus debe resolver un problema específico. Si no, es un bonus débil.

❌ Débil: "Bonus: Resource Library" (¿qué recursos? ¿valor de qué?)
✓ Fuerte: "Bonus: 50 Email Templates to Get Your First 10 Customers in 30 Days" (específico, resultado, timeframe)

**Rule 2: Each Bonus Must Be Valuable**

Si el cliente no vería valor en comprarlo por separado, no debería ser bonus.

**Rule 3: The "Bonus > Core" Technique**

Aquí está el secreto: Haz tus bonuses tan valiosos que SOLOS justifiquen el precio.

Ejemplo:
- Core: 6 coaching calls ($2k real value)
- Bonus 1: Done-For-You Sales Script Library ($500 real value)
- Bonus 2: 5 Personal Sales Call Reviews ($1,500 real value)
- Bonus 3: Done-For-You LinkedIn System Setup ($1,000 real value)

Si solo cobras los bonuses = $3,000
Pero incluyes el core GRATIS = cliente percibe increíble valor

---

### Bonus Naming Formula

**[Specific Result] + [Timeframe] = Powerful Bonus Name**

**❌ Weak naming:**
- "Resource Package"
- "Training Module"
- "Bonus Materials"
- "Extra Content"

**✓ Strong naming:**
- "The 30-Day Lead Generation Shortcut"
- "Your First $10k Customers Script"
- "The Week 1 Quick-Start Checklist"
- "The Done-For-You Email Sequence Library"

**Pattern:** Result + Timeframe + Specificity

---

### Types of Bonuses

#### 1. Tools & Templates (Perceived value: HIGH | Actual cost: LOW)

Estos son los mejores bonuses porque cuesta muy poco crearlos (si ya existen).

**Ejemplos:**
- Email templates (50+ pre-written sequences)
- Spreadsheets (lead tracker, financial model, scorecard)
- Checklists (daily action list, launch checklist)
- Scripts (sales scripts, objection responses)
- Slides/Presentations (pitch deck template, proposal template)

**Why they work:** Cliente ve $500-$2,000 value pero te costó $0 crear.

#### 2. Access (Perceived value: MEDIUM-HIGH | Actual cost: VARIABLE)

**Ejemplos:**
- Private community access (Slack, Circle, Facebook group)
- Monthly group calls (extended beyond program)
- Mastermind with other clients
- Expert Q&A sessions
- Live workshops (group)
- Event ticket access

#### 3. Speed/Quick-Win (Perceived value: HIGH | Actual cost: MEDIUM)

**Ejemplos:**
- 30-day quick start guide
- Done-for-you setup session
- Personal review of first 3 attempts
- Weekly accountability calls (first month free)
- Daily check-in text messages
- Priority support (email answered in 24h)

#### 4. Risk Reversal (Perceived value: VERY HIGH | Actual cost: NONE if positioned right)

**Ejemplos:**
- Money-back guarantee (if you don't get X result)
- Extra coaching if you don't get results
- Free continuation if goals not met
- "Refund if you don't use it for 30 days"

## 4. Guarantee Stack

### The Power of Guarantees

Una guarantee es quizás el elemento SINGLE más importante de tu oferta porque:
- Elimina ALL risk del lado del cliente
- Lo hace IMPOSSIBLY easy decir "sí"
- Demuestra confidence en tu producto
- Reduce refund rates (porque people are LESS likely to refund something guaranteed)

### Types of Guarantees

#### 1. Unconditional Money-Back Guarantee

**Estructura:** "Not satisfied? Money back, no questions asked."

**Detalles:**
- Full refund within 60 days
- No questions asked
- Keep all bonuses

**Cuándo usar:** Cuando tienes un producto/servicio con track record demostrado

**Copy ejemplo:**
```
60-Day "Get Your Money Back" Guarantee

Try the entire program risk-free for 60 days. If for any reason
you don't think it's worth it, just ask for a refund.
You keep all the bonuses. No questions asked.

We're confident because 97% of people who implement this get results.
```

#### 2. Conditional Guarantee

**Estructura:** "Do X. If you don't get result Y, money back."

**Detalles:**
- Cliente debe cumplir requisitos (completar programa, implementar estrategia, etc.)
- Si hace todo pero no obtiene resultado → refund

**Cuándo usar:** Cuando sabes que los RESULTADOS dependen de acción del cliente

**Copy ejemplo:**
```
"The 30-Day Implementation Guarantee"

Complete all 30 daily action items in the program.
If you do everything and don't land at least 3 new clients,
get 100% of your money back.

[Why this works: We've tracked 400+ people. Those who do the work get results.
Those who don't... well, they're refunding anyway.]
```

#### 3. Anti-Guarantee (Status/Exclusivity Models)

**Estructura:** "All sales final. Memberships are exclusive."

**Cuándo usar:** Para offers de alto-estatus, exclusividad, memberships premium
- Luxury coaching
- Mastermind groups
- Inner circles
- Exclusive communities

**Por qué funciona:** Scarcity + exclusivity + "you have to be serious" = más valor percibido

**Copy ejemplo:**
```
This is an invitation-only mastermind. Once accepted, membership is
non-refundable because: (1) we limit spots to maintain quality,
(2) we reject 80% of applicants, (3) commitment matters.
```

#### 4. Performance Guarantee

**Estructura:** "Get X result or refund + extra compensation."

**Detalles:**
- Cliente obtiene refund SI no logra el resultado
- PLUS: Extra compensation (bonus, free coaching, etc.)

**Cuándo usar:** Services de alto-ticket donde tienes MUCHA confidence

**Copy ejemplo:**
```
"The 6-Figure Revenue Guarantee"

We guarantee that by the end of 90 days, you'll have a clear
path to $100k ARR or we'll work with you for FREE for 3 more months
until you do, OR refund 100% of your investment.

We only make this guarantee because 94% of our clients hit $100k+
in their first year.
```

#### 5. Reverse Risk Guarantee

**Estructura:** "If it works, you pay. If it doesn't, we pay."

**Cuándo usar:** Rare; solo si tienes confidence EXTREMA y modelo de negocio lo permite

**Copy ejemplo:**
```
Performance-Based Pricing

We charge you 20% of new revenue we generate.
If we don't generate revenue, we work for free.
Zero risk for you.
```

#### 6. Stacked Guarantees

**Estructura:** Múltiples garantías en capas = perceived value MUY alta

**Ejemplo:**
```
Triple Guarantee

1. The "Try It Free" Guarantee
   30 days to access everything. No charge.

2. The "Results or Refund" Guarantee
   If you do the work and don't get results, 100% refund.

3. The "Extra Months Free" Guarantee
   If you want to continue but want free bonus coaching, we throw in
   2 extra months free.
```

---

### How to Choose the Right Guarantee

**Decision Matrix:**

| Guarantee Type | Best For | Risk Level |
|---|---|---|
| Unconditional | Digital products, courses, coaching with track record | Low-Medium |
| Conditional | Coaching, services requiring client action | Medium |
| Performance | High-ticket DFY services | Medium-High |
| Anti-Guarantee | Luxury, mastermind, exclusivity | Very Low |
| Reverse Risk | Done-For-You agencies, sales services | High |

---

### The "Bigger Guarantee = Bigger Conversions" Principle

**Data point:** When a coaching company moved from "60-day refund" to "fully unconditional" + "keep bonuses", refunds went DOWN 15% while conversions went UP 28%.

**Por qué:**
- Bigger guarantee = bigger confidence = more perceived value
- People who are serious enough to buy won't refund anyway
- Refund-seekers aren't your ideal customers

## 5. Scarcity & Urgency (REAL, Not Fake)

### The Critical Rule

**FAKE scarcity kills trust. And trust is everything.**

No hagas esto:
❌ "Only 5 spots available!" (entonces abres 10 más la próxima semana)
❌ "Today only! $500 off!" (pero lo repites cada semana)
❌ "Enrollment closes Friday!" (pero reabre Monday)

---

### The 4 Types of REAL Scarcity

#### 1. Limited Quantity

**La realidad:** Solo puedes servir X clientes bien.

**Cómo funciona:**
- Decides: "Max 10 clients por cohort"
- Sí lo cumples SIEMPRE
- La próxima cohort es en 6 semanas

**Copy:**
```
"We limit every cohort to 12 clients maximum because we want to
provide personalized feedback and ensure success.

Current spots available: 3 remaining.

Next cohort: June 1st (if you're interested, apply now)"
```

**Why it works:** Es real. People feel the real constraint.

#### 2. Limited Time (Enrollment Window)

**La realidad:** Tu programa tiene fechas de inicio/fin.

**Cómo funciona:**
- Enrollment: April 1-14
- Program starts: April 15
- Program ends: July 15
- Next cohort starts: August 1

**Copy:**

```
Only 3 weeks to enroll. Spots fill fast.
```

**Why it works:** Real constraint. Crea FOMO genuina.

#### 3. Limited Bonus (Bonuses Expire, Not the Offer)

**La realidad:** Los bonuses tienen fecha de expiración. La oferta base no.

**Esto es SUPER important:** NO digas que la oferta completa desaparece. Eso es fake.

**Di:** Los bonuses desaparecen.

**Copy:**
```

Limited-Time Bonuses (expire April 14):
- $1,000 "Done-For-You" Setup Session
- $500 "Scripts & Templates" Package
- $2,000 "First 3 Client Calls Review"

Total value: $3,500 in bonuses.

These bonuses are only available if you enroll before April 14.
After that, you get the core program but lose the bonuses.

Program base price: $997
With bonuses: $997 (save $3,500)
```

#### 4. Price Increase (Grandfather Pricing)

**La realidad:** Tu offer gets more valuable, so price goes up. Early customers get locked-in pricing.

**Cómo funciona:**
- Today: $997
- After 3rd cohort: $1,497
- Lifetime: Lock in at $997

**Copy:**
```
Grandfather Pricing Program

Enroll now: Lock in $997 for life.

This price increases to $1,497 after 10 people join (we're at 7).
Once it increases, anyone new pays the higher price.
But if you enroll now, you keep $997 forever.

[Countdown: 3 more spots at current price]
```

## 6. M-A-G-I-C Offer Naming Formula

### The Framework

The best offer names follow the **M-A-G-I-C formula:**

- **M** = Make a Magnetic Reason Why (por qué THIS? por qué AHORA?)
- **A** = Announce Your Avatar (quién es esto para)
- **G** = Give Them a Goal (el resultado específico)
- **I** = Indicate a Time Interval (cuánto toma)
- **C** = Complete with a Container Word (tipo de programa)

---

### Breaking Down Each Component

#### M = Magnetic Reason Why

This is THE hook. Why should someone even listen to your offer name?

**Palabras potentes:**
- "The Only..."
- "The Proven..."
- "The Done-For-You..."
- "The Exact..."
- "The Shortcut to..."
- "The [Surprising] Way to..."

**Ejemplos:**
- "The Proven Way to..." (magnetic: proven)
- "The Only Done-For-You..." (magnetic: done-for-you)
- "The Exact 90-Day..." (magnetic: exact)

#### A = Announce Your Avatar

Who is this FOR? Say it clearly.

**❌ Vague:** "The Marketing Mastery Program"
**✓ Clear:** "The Agency Owner's Revenue Machine"

**Ejemplos de avatars:**
- "For Coaches"
- "For SaaS Founders"
- "For Agencies"
- "For E-Commerce Sellers"
- "For Small Business Owners"
- "For Fitness Professionals"

#### G = Give Them a Goal

What's the RESULT? Not the process; the outcome.

**❌ Process-focused:** "Learn Email Marketing"
**✓ Result-focused:** "Generate 10 New Clients in 30 Days"

**Ejemplos:**
- "Land 5 New High-Ticket Clients"
- "Double Your Revenue"
- "Build a Profitable Team"
- "Get 100 New Leads"

#### I = Indicate a Time Interval

How fast? This is CRUCIAL for perceived value.

**Ejemplos:**
- "In 30 Days"
- "In 90 Days"
- "In 12 Weeks"
- "In Your First 6 Months"

**Nota:** Timeframe más corto = más urgencia = más valor

#### C = Complete with a Container Word

What's the FORMAT? One of:
- Program, Course, Accelerator, Bootcamp, Intensive
- Blueprint, System, Framework, Method
- Playbook, Roadmap, Toolkit
- Machine, Engine, Formula
- Mastermind, Circle, Cohort
- Sprint, Challenge, Immersion

## 7. Value Ladder & Ascension Model

### The Concept

No sells one customer $X once. You build a RELATIONSHIP escalator.

```
                  ↑ Continuity ($1-5k/month)
                  |
                  ↑ High-Ticket ($5k-50k)
                  |
                  ↑ Mid-Ticket ($1k-$5k)
                  |
                  ↑ Low-Ticket ($100-$1k)
                  |
                  ↑ Free (Lead Magnet/Funnel)
```

Cada peldaño alimenta el siguiente. El objetivo: Maximizar customer lifetime value (CLTV).

### The Free Layer (Top of Funnel)

**Propósito:** Atrae a tu avatar y demuestra expertise.

**Típicamente:**
- Blog post/article
- Lead magnet (checklist, guide, template, quiz)
- Free training/webinar
- Free tool or resource
- Social media content

**Regla:** Debe ser INSANELY valuable. No salvo lo mejor para algo de pago.

**Ejemplo: Coaching Business**
```
Free: "The Perfect Client Profile Workbook"
- 20 questions to identify your ideal avatar
- Segmentation template
- Messaging framework starter

Valor: La mayoría de coaches no hace esto.
Resultado: Descifran quién vender, y eso vale MILES.
```

### The Low-Ticket Layer ($100-$1,000)

**Propósito:** Convertir free audience → paying customers (de bajo precio)

**Típicamente:**
- Small courses ($97-$497)
- Mini-programs (30 days, 6 weeks)
- Templates/playbooks ($47-$297)
- Entry-level coaching (group, limited)
- Low-ticket group programs

**Estrategia:** Make it EASY para que alguien compre.
- Bajo precio = baja fricción
- Sin garantía complicada necesaria
- Fast delivery (digital es ideal)

**Ejemplo: Agency Owner Coach**
```
Low-Ticket: "The $5k Client Proposal Templates" ($297)
- 15 pre-built proposal templates
- Customization guide
- Pricing strategies
- Pitching framework

Cliente toma: "Okay, this person knows what they're doing"
→ Ahora está OPEN to higher offers
```

### The Mid-Ticket Layer ($1,000-$5,000)

**Propósito:** Deliver serious results, gather proof, deepen relationship

**Típicamente:**
- 8-12 week group programs
- Small group coaching (5-20 people)
- Done-with-you services (combined effort)
- Intensive workshops
- Certification programs

**Estrategia:** Ahora puedes ofrecer personal interaction + more value
- Include coaching/feedback
- Más personalized approach
- Créate case studies and proof

**Ejemplo:**
```
Mid-Ticket: "The 12-Week Coaching Accelerator" ($2,997)
- 12 group coaching calls
- Done-with-you offer redesign
- Sales system setup
- Community access
- Personal feedback on 3 calls

Resultado: Clients get tangible wins, you get testimonials/case studies
```

### The High-Ticket Layer ($5,000-$50,000+)

**Propósito:** White-glove service. 1-to-1 attention. Premium results.

**Típicamente:**
- 1-on-1 coaching (6+ months)
- Done-for-you services (full agency work)
- Private masterminds
- Executive coaching
- Premium consulting

**Estrategia:** Solo habla con people who have proven they're serious (via mid-tier purchase)

**Ejemplo:**
```
High-Ticket: "The 6-Month Revenue Scaling Program" ($15k-$25k)
- 2 calls per week (1-on-1)
- Done-for-you system setup
- Sales/marketing execution
- 24/7 support (priority)
- Quarterly strategic planning

Candidatos: People who already bought the $2,997 program + got results
```

### The Continuity Layer ($1,000-$5,000/month)

**Propósito:** Ongoing relationship + recurring revenue for you

**Típicamente:**
- Mastermind/community membership
- Monthly coaching subscription
- Retainer-based services
- Group coaching circles
- Advanced programs

**Estrategia:** Only for people who've experienced RESULTS with you already

**Ejemplo:**
```
Continuity: "The Elite Coaching Circle" ($2,000/month)
- Monthly group masterminds
- Private Slack community
- Marketing templates + updates
- Monthly 1-on-1 strategy call
- Exclusive events 2x/year

Candidatos: People who've had wins with you + want ongoing growth
```

---

### The Value Ladder Feeding Strategy

Each layer should PROMOTE the next layer.

```
Free Layer: "Check out my $297 templates to take it further"
   ↓
Low-Ticket: "Join the group program to implement this with support"
   ↓
Mid-Ticket: "Ready for 1-on-1? Let's talk high-ticket"
   ↓
High-Ticket: "Want ongoing support? Join the elite circle"
   ↓
Continuity: ∞
```

**Specifically HOW to promote:**

1. **In every free piece:** "If you want me to implement this FOR you, [Link to low-ticket]"
2. **At end of low-ticket:** "Get 1-on-1 coaching + done-for-you in [mid-ticket]"
3. **During mid-ticket:** "If you want white-glove service, apply for [high-ticket]"
4. **With high-ticket clients:** "Stay with me in the [continuity] mastermind"

---

### The "Goodwill" Model

Alex Hormozi's philosophy: **"Give away your best stuff free. Charge for implementation."**

**Cómo funciona:**

```
Free: Your BEST content/framework
  "Here's how to structure an offer"

Low-Ticket: Your templates so they can do it themselves
  "Here are 50 templates you can customize"

Mid-Ticket: Group implementation + feedback
  "We'll implement together with coaching"

High-Ticket: Done-for-you implementation
  "We do it all, you just approve"
```

**Why this works:**
- People don't pay for information; they pay for RESULTS
- By giving free info, you prove you know what you're talking about
- People who really want to do it themselves buy templates
- People who want support buy coaching
- People who want done-for-you buy premium

**No gatekeeping.**

### Lifetime Customer Value (CLTV) Calculation

Maximizing CLTV es la razón para un value ladder.

**Formula:**
```
CLTV = (Low-ticket customers × avg price) +
       (Low→Mid conversion rate × Mid-ticket price) +
       (Mid→High conversion rate × High-ticket price) +
       (High→Continuity conversion rate × Continuity × months)
```

**Ejemplo:**
```
100 people buy low-ticket ($297) = $29,700

30% convert to mid-ticket ($2,497) = $22,473

20% of mid convert to high ($15,000) = $90,000 (for 6 people)

40% of high stay in continuity ($2k/month × 12 months) = $48,000

TOTAL CLTV FROM 100 CUSTOMERS = $189,173
Average per customer: $1,891.73

vs. If you only had high-ticket:
3-5 customers at $15k = $45k-$75k only
(Much lower!)
```
