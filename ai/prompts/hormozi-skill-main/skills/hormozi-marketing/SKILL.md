# Hormozi Marketing Asset Creator

Create every marketing asset a business needs using Alex Hormozi's battle-tested frameworks from three books: **$100M Offers**, **$100M Leads**, and **Gym Launch Secrets**. Every piece of copy, every offer, every funnel component must follow these principles — no generic marketing allowed.

## Core Philosophy

Hormozi's entire system rests on one idea: **make the offer so good people feel stupid saying no**. Every asset created with this skill must pass that test. If it doesn't, it's not done.

Three rules that override everything else:

1. **Specificity beats cleverness.** "10 clients in 30 days" beats "grow your business." Numbers, timeframes, and concrete outcomes always.
2. **The customer is the hero.** Every word must be about THEIR result, THEIR transformation, THEIR dream outcome. Never about the company.
3. **Remove risk, remove friction, remove time.** The Value Equation drives every decision.

---

## The Value Equation (Foundation of Everything)

Before creating ANY asset, apply the Value Equation:

```
         Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement
Value = ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
         Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice
```

Every marketing asset must:
- **Maximize the Dream Outcome** — paint the biggest credible transformation
- **Maximize Perceived Likelihood** — stack proof, guarantees, credentials
- **Minimize Time Delay** — show speed of results
- **Minimize Effort & Sacrifice** — make it feel easy, done-for-you, frictionless

When reviewing or creating ANY copy, score each quadrant 1-10 and improve the weakest.

## Universal Output Rules

Apply these to EVERY asset created:

### Always Do
- Use specific numbers: quantities, percentages, timeframes, dollar amounts
- Write in first or second person ("I" / "you") — never third person
- Keep sentences under 15 words maximum
- One idea per sentence
- Start hooks with the most provocative word
- Include at least one concrete proof point (testimonial, case study, metric)
- End every section on a peak of emotion or curiosity
- Single CTA per asset — one action, one button, one link
- Guarantee that reverses ALL risk, not just some

### Never Do
- Use corporate jargon: "solution", "leverage", "synergy", "optimize"
- Write vague claims: "high quality", "best in class", "industry leading"
- Talk about features without connecting to outcomes
- Use "we" as the subject — the copy is about the customer
- Use fake urgency or manufactured scarcity
- Write CTAs with multiple options
- Include filler phrases: "in today's world", "at the end of the day"

### Prohibited Phrases
"Solución integral", "De calidad", "Expertos en el rubro", "Te ayudamos a crecer", "Resultados garantizados" (without specifying what), "El mejor del mercado", "Podemos ayudarte", "No te pierdas esta oportunidad", "We're passionate about", "Our mission is", "Click here", "Buy now" (without context), "Act fast", "Limited time" (without real reason)
